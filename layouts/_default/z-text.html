The one idea

Other messengers run on company servers. Z-TEXT runs on BitcoinZ nodes worldwide.

Signal, WhatsApp, and Telegram all pass your messages through servers their company runs. Z-TEXT has no central server in the message path. A message is encrypted on your device and broadcast as a shielded transaction to the BitcoinZ network, where independent nodes spread across the world relay it, the same way BTCZ itself is sent.

On-chain channels

Reach everyone for the price of one transaction

A channel is a shared mailbox on the blockchain. You post once and every subscriber's app reads it. The cost is the same for 10 followers or 10,000, and reading is always free.

  • Impossible to deplatform. There is no central server to seize and no account to suspend.
  • No subscriber list to leak. zk-SNARKs hide who posted, and no central database of followers exists.
  • You own your audience. Channels and their history restore from your seed on any device.
