Community member **RGBTCZ** has released **BTCZ Tools** - a free desktop toolkit that puts everything a BitcoinZ miner checks daily into one clean app. There is now a standalone Windows .exe: just download and double-click. No Python, no setup.

<!--more-->

## What's inside

Eight modules, built on a shared data layer - a piece of network data fetched once is reused everywhere:

- **Dashboard** - network, your mining (today / 7d / 30d), profit and price at a glance
- **Mining Tracker** - rewards per day or date range, CSV export, live pool-side stats
- **Profitability** - revenue, cost, profit, break-even and ROI, with GPU presets so you can pick your card and quantity
- **Pool Explorer** - on-chain pool distribution plus live stats from zpool, SW Groupe, Dark Fiber Mines and HimPool
- **Network Explorer** - network stats and the latest blocks, each with the miner that found it
- **History** - monthly history, projections and alerts
- **Mining Assistant** - analyzes your setup and gives honest, actionable advice

![BTCZ Tools Mining Assistant](/images/mining/btcz-tools/screenshot-mining-assistant.png)

It ships in **four languages** - English, French, Spanish and German - switchable on the fly from the sidebar.

## Free and open source

BTCZ Tools is released under the **MIT license**, so anyone can read the code, run it from source, or contribute. Huge thanks to the pool operators who shared their APIs to make the live pool statistics possible. Feedback and pull requests are welcome.
