## About Z-TEXT

Z-TEXT is the official BitcoinZ wallet. It is stable, fast, and secure, and it pairs a self-custodial BTCZ wallet with a private messenger that runs on the BitcoinZ blockchain.

Your wallet comes from a 24-word seed that only you hold. There is no account to register, no phone number, and no email.

### Wallet

- **Shielded payments**: BTCZ is sent as BitcoinZ shielded transactions, with sender, recipient, and amount hidden by zk-SNARKs.
- **Self-custodial**: your keys stay on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
- **Seed recovery**: restore your wallet on any device with your 24-word seed.
- **Pay inside a chat**: attach a BTCZ payment to a conversation.
- **No license needed**: sending and receiving BTCZ is always available.

### Messenger

- **Private by design**: no phone number, no email, and no central message server.
- **Two layers of encryption**: AES-256-GCM on your device, then a BitcoinZ shielded transaction.
- **Fast delivery**: messages typically arrive in 1 to 5 seconds.
- **Restores from your seed**: your conversations and contacts come back on any device.

Sending messages requires a Z-TEXT license. Receiving messages and all wallet functions do not. See [Z-TEXT packages](https://z-text.com/packages).

## Download Safely

Z-TEXT is available for **Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux**.

**Only download Z-TEXT from [z-text.com/downloads](https://z-text.com/downloads).** A wallet holds your keys, so a copy from another website or a third-party APK mirror can be altered to steal funds.

Each build on the download page shows its version and a SHA-256 checksum. After downloading, check that the checksum of your file matches the one shown there.
