## About the Electrum Web Wallet

The BitcoinZ Electrum Web Wallet is the Electrum light wallet as a web app. Open [electrum.btcz.rocks](https://electrum.btcz.rocks/) in your browser and you can create a new wallet or import an existing one. There is nothing to download and no blockchain to sync.

### What you can do

- Create a new wallet or import one you already have
- Send and receive BTCZ
- See your balance and transaction history
- Save addresses you use often in the address book
- Scan QR codes with your camera
- Switch language, display currency, and light or dark theme

## Keep Your Seed Phrase Safe

Your wallet is saved in your browser, protected by the password you set.

1. Write down your seed phrase when you create the wallet and keep it offline
2. Never share your seed phrase with anyone
3. Clearing your browser data removes the wallet from that browser, so make sure you have the seed phrase first
4. To use the wallet on another device, import it there with your seed phrase

## Use It Safely

A web wallet is loaded from a website every time you open it, so the address matters.

- Only use **electrum.btcz.rocks** and check the address bar before you type a password or seed phrase
- Bookmark the page instead of following links from messages or search ads
- Use a device and browser you trust
